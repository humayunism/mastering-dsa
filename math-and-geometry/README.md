## Key Concepts to Remember

- **Efficiency**: Many math problems can be solved with O(1) or O(log n) time complexity using mathematical formulas
- **Modular Arithmetic**: Essential for handling large numbers and preventing overflow
- **Bit Manipulation**: Often used for efficient mathematical operations
- **Precision**: Be careful with floating-point arithmetic in geometry problems
- **Edge Cases**: Consider negative numbers, zero, and boundary conditions

---

## Tips for Solving Math Problems

1. **Understand the Problem**: Break down mathematical concepts into algorithmic steps
2. **Look for Patterns**: Many problems have mathematical patterns or formulas
3. **Consider Constraints**: Think about time and space complexity for large inputs
4. **Use Built-in Functions**: Leverage language-specific math libraries when appropriate
5. **Test Thoroughly**: Math problems often have tricky edge cases
